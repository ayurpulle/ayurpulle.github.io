I’m a physicist moving into technical AI safety, with a background in quantum computing.

I’m doing an MSc in Physics and Quantum Dynamics at Imperial College London, after a First in Physics with Theoretical Physics there. In summer 2026 I was a research fellow at Dovetail, working on agent foundations under an ARIA grant: what a competent agent must know about its world. In October 2026 I took part in the Iliad Intensive in San Francisco.

Before that I worked on quantum metrology with GKP states in linear optics, and wrote my bachelor’s thesis on modular architectures for trapped-ion and neutral-atom quantum computers. I’m mostly interested in mechanistic interpretability and the mathematics of agents and learning.
